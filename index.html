Learning to Drive

Final year project · Abdelrahman Tantavy · BSc Mathematics & Computer Science · University of Birmingham, 2026–27

The idea

The goal of this project is to train a car in the CARLA driving simulator to drive itself from a start point to a destination in a city, avoiding obstacles, stopping at red lights and dealing with other cars and pedestrians.

To do this I'm using reinforcement learning: the car tries things, gets a reward for good driving (making progress, staying in lane) and a penalty for bad driving (crashing, running red lights), and slowly learns what works. The learning algorithm I plan to use is PPO.

I'm new to machine learning, so the first weeks are about learning the basics, then building up one step at a time, starting with empty roads and adding difficulty gradually. At the end I'll compare my car with a normal rule-based driver and test it on maps it hasn't seen before.

How it works

1. LookThe car reads simple information: its speed, the road ahead and how far away objects are.
2. DecideThe AI (a neural network) chooses how much to steer, accelerate or brake.
3. ActCARLA moves the car and updates the world.
4. ScoreThe car gets points or loses points, and PPO uses the score to improve the AI.

This loop repeats many thousands of times during training.
