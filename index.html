Learning to Drive

A simulated car that teaches itself to drive from A to B across a city, avoiding obstacles, other vehicles and pedestrians, and stopping at red lights. It learns by trial and error with PPO, and is judged against a hand-written, rule-based driver on towns it has never seen.

Research questions

  1. Can a PPO agent using simple sensor readings (not camera images) reliably reach its destination in a simulated city?
  2. Does training in stages of increasing difficulty (a curriculum) make learning faster or better?
  3. How does the learned driver compare with CARLA's rule-based driver on success, safety and rule-following?
  4. Does the driver still work in towns it never trained in?

    03 · How it works

    The learning loop

    Twenty times a simulated second, the car reads its surroundings, the policy network picks a steering and pedal command, CARLA moves the world forward, and the reward function scores what happened. PPO uses those scores to adjust the network.

    PartDesignWhy

    Training curriculum

    The agent moves to the next stage once it reaches 80% success on the current one.
